This policy explains what personal information Noor Jeddah Typing & Documents Photocopying ("we", "us", "our") collects from its customers, why we collect it, how we protect it, and what rights you have over it. 3. Where the information comes from

All of it comes from you, or from someone acting for you — usually when you visit our office, send us documents, or message us. We do not buy contact lists, and we do not add people who have never been our customers.

4. Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To carry out the work you asked us to do. We cannot process a renewal without your documents and details.
  • To remind you before a document expires. Expired documents in the UAE can lead to fines. Reminding you is part of the service we provide, and it is in your interest and ours.
  • To answer you when you contact us.
  • To keep proper business records, where the law requires us to.

5. The WhatsApp reminders

If you are our customer and you have given us your mobile number, we may send you reminder messages on WhatsApp before and after one of your documents expires. These messages are service reminders about your own documents. They are not advertising.

Who sends them

Reminders are sent from our official reminder number, +971 56 735 6642. Every message includes our business name and our main contact number.

The technology we use

We send these messages using the WhatsApp Business Platform, which is operated by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited. To deliver a message, your mobile number is passed to Meta. Meta's own privacy policy governs how they handle it. We store your details in Google Sheets, provided by Google, which we use as our customer record system. Both companies act as service providers to us and are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes.

How to stop them

Reply STOP to any reminder, or call or message us on +971 56 735 6642, and we will stop sending reminders to your number. Please note that if you stop reminders, you remain responsible for renewing your documents on time.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for marketing.

We share it only in these cases:

  • UAE government authorities and their approved systems, where this is necessary to process the application you asked us to make on your behalf.
  • Service providers who help us operate, such as Meta for WhatsApp delivery and Google for storing records. They may only act on our instructions.
  • Where the law requires it, such as a lawful request from an authority or a court.

7. Where your information is stored

Your information is stored electronically using services that may hold data on servers outside the United Arab Emirates. Where information is transferred abroad, we use established providers who apply recognised safeguards and contractual protections.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as you remain our customer, and afterwards for as long as the law requires us to keep business records. When we no longer need your information, we delete it or remove anything that identifies you. If you ask us to delete your information, we will do so unless we are legally required to keep it.

9. How we protect it

  • Access is limited to the staff who need it for their work.
  • Our systems are protected by passwords and, where available, two-step verification.
  • Automated systems connect to our records using restricted accounts that can only read and write what they need.
  • We do not keep account passwords or login details of customers in shared files.

No system can be completely secure, but we take these measures seriously and review them.

10. Your rights

Under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data, you may:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you, and receive a copy.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we are not required to keep it.
  • Ask us to stop sending you WhatsApp reminders.
  • Object to how we use your information, or ask us to limit that use.
  • Withdraw your consent, where we relied on it.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 12. We will respond within a reasonable period and will not charge you for a normal request.

11. Children

Our services are for adults and for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Where we process documents relating to a child — for example a family visa — we do so only on the instruction of that child's parent or legal guardian.
